What happens next.

TestFlight is free, and it’s made by Apple.

Step 1

Look for an email from TestFlight

The invite comes from Apple’s TestFlight, sent to the email you left here. It can take a little while, so check your junk folder too.

Step 2

Get the TestFlight app

On your iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the invite email and tap View in TestFlight.

Step 3

Install HumFut

Tap Accept, then Install. HumFut appears on your Home Screen like any app, and TestFlight tells you when there’s a new build.

Beta questions.

Why do you need my name and Apple Account email?

TestFlight invites are tied to an email. Using the one you sign in to the App Store with means the invite opens straight into TestFlight on your iPhone. TestFlight also lists each tester by name, so we know who’s who.

Does it cost anything?

No. TestFlight is free, and in-app purchases in a TestFlight build aren’t charged. They also don’t carry over to the App Store version.

How do I send feedback?

Take a screenshot in HumFut and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. You can also email humfut-support@nullbytes.app.

What does TestFlight share with us?

Apple shows us your tester email and name, when you installed and opened the beta, and any crash reports or feedback you send through TestFlight. More in the Privacy Policy

How long does a beta build last?

Each TestFlight build works for 90 days. Newer builds arrive in TestFlight before then.

Can I keep the App Store version too?

No. The beta replaces the App Store version of HumFut on your iPhone while you test. To go back, leave the beta and install HumFut from the App Store again.

How do I leave the beta?

Open TestFlight, choose HumFut and tap Stop Testing, then install HumFut from the App Store again. To have your email removed from our list, write to humfut-support@nullbytes.app.
