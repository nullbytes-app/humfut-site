Privacy Policy

HumFut: Sounds for Calls · nullBytes · Effective September 26, 2026

In one sentence: HumFut does not collect your data. Everything you type, import, capture or save stays on your iPhone (and your paired Apple Watch, if you use one). We have no servers, no analytics, and no way to see any of it.

What HumFut does

HumFut lets you speak typed phrases and play sounds into a live call, using Apple's microphone-injection capability. It never places a call itself, never uses your microphone, never records a call, and never listens to one. A separate "Capture" feature lets you record audio playing in another app on your own device, using Apple's ReplayKit broadcast picker, entirely under your control.

Data we collect

None. HumFut contains no networking code of any kind — no URLSession, no analytics software development kit, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code libraries that could add any of these behind the scenes. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

Specifically, HumFut does not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your voice, your microphone, or any call audio
  • The phrases you type or the sounds you play
  • Contacts, location, photos, or any other device data
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or advertising identifiers

What stays on your device, and where

HumFut stores everything it needs in a private storage area shared only between the app and its Share Extension and Capture Extension (Apple calls this an "App Group container"), plus ordinary on-device app storage:

  • Typed and saved phrases (Speak) are saved in the App Group container on your device.
  • Imported and captured audio — files you import from Files or share in from another app, and audio you capture with the Capture feature — is saved in the App Group container on your device. Capture only ever reads the app audio it is shown; it discards any video or microphone data it receives without saving it.
  • Your settings (such as mix levels and Call Sounds preferences) are saved on-device using Apple's standard app-storage system (UserDefaults).
  • A small counter of your free trial calls is kept in the device Keychain, scoped to this device only, so it can survive a reinstall the way an in-app purchase entitlement does. It never leaves your device and is never synced to iCloud Keychain.

None of this data is uploaded to us, to Apple, or to any other party. Deleting HumFut removes the app's on-device storage; the Keychain-held free-call counter, described above, is the one item designed to persist across a reinstall on the same device.

Apple Watch

If you use the HumFut Apple Watch app, it communicates with your iPhone only through Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity framework — a direct link between your own paired devices. This is used to keep your sound library and board in sync and to relay playback commands (like play, stop, and adjust level) between your iPhone and Watch. This data travels only between your own two devices; it never reaches nullBytes or anyone else.

In-app purchases

The one-time "Unlock" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. HumFut never sees your payment details — Apple processes the transaction and tells the app only whether you're entitled to the unlock. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles purchase information.

No accounts, no tracking

HumFut has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no way to identify you. We do not use tracking technologies, and we do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share.

Children's privacy

HumFut is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what this policy describes, we'll update this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of HumFut after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how HumFut handles data can be sent to ravi@nullbytes.app.
