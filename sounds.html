Where to find sounds

Find a sound. Share it to HumFut.

Many sound sites have a Share button under each sound. Tap it, choose HumFut, and the sound is yours to put on a Pad.

Three taps from a website to a Pad.

Use Safari or Chrome on your iPhone, with HumFut already installed.

Step 1

Search for a soundboard

In Safari or Chrome, search for soundboard, sound buttons or sound effects buttons. Add what you’re after to narrow it down, like “funny soundboard” or “applause sound button”. Open a site that shows a play button for each sound.

Step 2

Tap Share under the sound

Most sound sites show a play button for each sound, with a Download icon and a Share icon under it. Tap the sound’s own Share icon, not the browser’s, so the audio file goes with it.

Step 3

Choose HumFut

Find HumFut in the row of apps. A card says “Got it” and closes by itself. Open HumFut: the sound is in Library, marked New. Tap + to put it on a Pad.

Only a Download button?

Tap Download to save the sound to Files. Then in HumFut, go to Library, tap Add a sound and pick it from Downloads.

Short sounds work best

Up to 30 seconds becomes a Clip you can put on a Pad. Anything longer becomes a Track, for background music. Hold a Pad to rename it, pick an emoji or trim it.

    If it doesn’t work.

    HumFut doesn’t show up when I tap Share.

    HumFut appears when what you share is an audio file. If you only see apps for links, you’re sharing the page, not the sound: use the Share icon right under the sound, not the browser’s Share button. If HumFut still isn’t in the row, swipe to the end and tap More. If HumFut says “Open HumFut once to finish setting up, then share this again.”, do just that.

    I shared a sound but it isn’t in my Library.

    Shared sounds finish adding when you next open HumFut. Open the app and look in Library for the sound marked New.

    Why won’t HumFut add a sound?

    HumFut checks every sound on your iPhone and won’t add one that sounds like an emergency siren, an automated phone menu or dial tones, or a phone ringtone, because playing it into a call could be taken as real. Choose another sound.

    The site has no Share or Download button.

    Then it doesn’t offer the sound as a file. Look for another site that does.
