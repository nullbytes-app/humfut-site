HumFut: Sounds for Calls · nullBytes

HumFut lets you type what you want to say and have it spoken into a live Phone or FaceTime call, for moments when speaking out loud isn't an option. You can also drop in reactions, your own sounds, and background music, all mixed into the same call.

It works on an iPhone running iOS 26 or later, during a live Phone or FaceTime call, using the "Microphone Injection" permission Apple built for adding audio to a call.

Adding your own sounds

There are three ways to get a sound into HumFut. Every sound lands in your Library first, and from there you can put it on a Pad. Watch the animated walkthroughs.

Share from a website or app

  1. Open the sound's page on a soundboard site in Chrome or Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose HumFut. A card confirms it was added.

Capture from another app

  1. In HumFut's Library, tap the capture button.
  2. Choose Start Sharing and wait for the 3-second countdown.
  3. Play the sound in the other app, then tap the red bar to stop.

Import from Files

  1. In your Library, tap Add a sound.
  2. Pick an audio file from Files.
  3. Clips are up to 30 seconds. Longer files can be Tracks.

To change a sound, hold its Pad: you can rename it, pick a colour or emoji, set where it starts and ends, or move it to another Collection.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later
  • A live Phone or FaceTime call (audio is only added while a call is actually connected)
  • The Microphone Injection permission, granted once during onboarding
  • Optional: a paired Apple Watch, for playing sounds and adjusting levels from your wrist

Frequently asked questions

I can't hear sounds on the call

Make sure Call Sounds is turned on and you're in a live Phone or FaceTime call — HumFut only adds audio to the call while one is actually connected. Check that you granted the Microphone Injection permission when asked (Settings → HumFut, if you need to review it). Also note that call audio is unavailable while you're screen sharing, and that ending the call disarms injection until your next call.

The call app muted me and now nothing plays

This is expected. HumFut's audio travels over the same call uplink your voice does, so muting the call also mutes anything HumFut adds. Unmute the call to hear HumFut again.

HumFut doesn't appear when I tap Share

HumFut appears in the share sheet when what you're sharing is an audio file. On a soundboard site, open the sound's own page and share from there. If HumFut says to open the app first, open HumFut once so it can finish setting up, then share again.

How long can a sound be?

A Clip for a Pad can be up to 30 seconds. A capture stops on its own after 60 seconds and HumFut keeps the first 30 seconds of sound, with silence trimmed from the ends. Longer audio can be added as a Track and played as background music.

Why didn't HumFut add my sound?

HumFut is for sounds that are obviously not real — airhorns, laugh tracks, sound clips, music. Sounds that imitate an emergency, an official, or a real person's voice can deceive the person on your call, so HumFut doesn't carry them. It also won't add sirens, phone-menu tones or ringtones, and it tells you why when it refuses a file.

How does background music work?

Import a longer audio file as a Track, then play it from the Track strip. You can pause it, stop it or loop it, and set two levels: how loud it is for the other person (They hear) and how loud it is for you (You hear).

What does the unlock buy?

New installs get 3 free calls with Call Sounds turned on, where the other person can hear you. After that, a single one-time purchase (with Family Sharing support) lets the other person hear you — Speak, Reactions and music alike — on every call after that, forever. Typing phrases, trying sounds on your own speaker, and editing your library and collections are always free, whether or not you've unlocked. You can restore a previous purchase from Settings → Unlock HumFut → Restore Purchases.

How do I add my own sounds, or capture audio from another app?

To add your own sounds: import an audio file from Files, or use the Share button in another app and choose HumFut. To capture audio playing in another app: open Capture in HumFut, start a broadcast using Apple's system picker, then play the sound in the other app — HumFut keeps the captured clip in your library with silence trimmed from the ends. See Adding your own sounds above for step-by-step guides.

Does it record my calls?

No. HumFut never uses your microphone, never records a call, and never listens to one. It only adds its own audio — your typed phrases, sounds and music — into the call using Apple's microphone-injection capability. "Capture" is a separate, user-started feature for capturing audio from another app on your own device; it has nothing to do with your calls.
