How can we help?

Answers for HumFut: Sounds for Calls, written from how the app actually works.

Get set up.

Three things, once. After that it’s call, Turn On, tap.

Step 1

An iPhone with iOS 26

HumFut runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. If an iPhone can’t add audio to calls, HumFut says so straight away: “This iPhone can’t do Call Sounds.”

Step 2

Allow what iOS asks for

The welcome screens walk you through the two things iOS has to allow: Injection in Settings, and Call audio, which HumFut asks for when you tap Allow call audio.

Say no and your Pads still play on your own phone. Nothing reaches a call until both are on. If one is off later, tap Open Settings in HumFut. It takes you straight to the Accessibility setting that lets apps add audio to calls.

Step 3

Call, then Turn On

Start a call the way you always do. When HumFut shows Supported Call detected., tap Turn On, then tap a Pad.

Call Sounds turn off by themselves when the call ends, so you turn them on for each call.

Get a sound in, then put it on a Pad.

Every sound lands in your Library first. Try each way on the iPhone

Share

From a soundboard site

  1. In Safari or Chrome, open the sound’s own page.
  2. Tap the sound’s Share button and choose HumFut.
  3. A card says “Got it. HumFut will finish adding this the next time you open the app.” It closes by itself.
  4. Open HumFut. The sound is in Library, marked New.

Capture

From a video or any app

  1. In Library, tap Capture, then the red record button.
  2. Your iPhone’s Screen Sharing card opens with HumFut Capture ticked. Tap Start Sharing.
  3. After the 3-second countdown, play the sound in any app.
  4. Tap the red indicator at the top of the screen, then Stop. Back in HumFut: “Captured. It’s in your Library marked New.”

Capture stops by itself at 60 seconds and keeps the first 30 seconds, with the silence trimmed.

Import

From your Files

  1. In Library, tap Add a sound.
  2. Pick an audio file in Files: Downloads, iCloud Drive or anywhere else.
  3. Up to 30 seconds becomes a Clip, marked New. Anything longer becomes a Track, for background music.

Put it on a Pad

In Library, under Clips, tap + next to the sound (Add to Reactions). It becomes a Pad on your Reactions board. Sounds not on a Pad yet say “Not on Reactions”.

Tracks play from Library and don’t go on a Pad.

Change a Pad

Hold a Pad to open Edit Reaction: its Name and Show name, then Background for a GIF, Look (Icon and Colour), Sound (Volume, and Play range for where it starts and ends), and Move to position and Move to Collection. At the top, Share (the box with an arrow) sends it to someone, and Remove (the trash can) takes it off Reactions.

Long-press a Collection header on Reactions to create a Collection. Rename, reorder, share or delete Collections in Settings → Manage Collections.

Put a GIF behind a Pad.

A Pad can show a GIF that plays along with its sound. GIF backgrounds are part of Unlock, and starter Pads can have one too.

Add

Pick a GIF

  1. Hold a Pad to open Edit Reaction.
  2. Under Background, tap Add GIF.
  3. Choose Photos, Files or Paste a GIF link…. Copied a GIF? Tap Paste next to it.

A link can be a direct .gif link, a GIPHY or Tenor share link, or a web page with a GIF in its preview. Only secure (https) links work, and a GIF can be up to 8 MB.

Frame

Choose what shows

Drag to move the GIF and pinch to zoom. The Pad shows what is inside the frame, on the Board exactly as in the sheet.

Key frame is the still the Pad rests on. Tap or drag along the strip to choose it, then tap Save. Reset goes back to the centre, the first frame and the start of the GIF; tap Save to keep that.

Timing

Match it to the sound

Drag the yellow window to choose which part of the GIF plays with the sound. The window is exactly as long as the sound. If the sound is longer than the GIF, the GIF loops to fit.

Tap Play with sound to try the GIF and sound together before you save.

How a GIF Pad plays

  • As it comes into view, the GIF plays once in slow motion, then rests on its key frame. It plays again only after it has left the screen and come back.
  • Tap it and the GIF plays at normal speed with the sound, with a flash of the Pad’s own colour, and stops when the sound stops.
  • Turn off Show name to hide the name on the Board only, so the GIF speaks for itself.

Change or remove it

Hold the Pad: Replace picks a different GIF, and Remove GIF takes it off. Your sound stays as it is.

The GIF is kept with the Pad. Remove the Pad and its GIF goes too.

Without Unlock, choosing a GIF opens Unlock GIF backgrounds.

Share your Reactions.

Send one Reaction or a whole Collection to a friend as a small .humfut file. They tap it, and it’s on their Reactions board.

Send

One Reaction

  1. Hold a Pad to open Edit Reaction.
  2. At the top, tap Share, the box with an arrow.
  3. Send it by Messages, AirDrop, Mail or any other app.

The first time you share, HumFut reminds you: “Only share sounds and GIFs you have the right to share.” Tap Continue.

Send

A whole Collection

  1. Go to Settings → Manage Collections.
  2. Under the Collection’s name, tap Share.
  3. Pick how to send it.

If the Collection still has an automatic name like “Collection B”, Name your Collection opens first with that name filled in, because the people who add it will see it. Change it and tap Share: your Collection takes the new name too. A Collection you’ve already named shares straight away.

A pack holds up to 12 Reactions, 60 seconds per sound and 50 MB. A bigger Collection says “That’s too big to share.”

Receive

Add what you’re sent

  1. Tap the .humfut file in Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files. Or tap Share on it in another app and choose HumFut.
  2. Add Reactions lists what’s inside. Reactions with a GIF have a GIF badge.
  3. For a Collection, you can change its name at the top.
  4. Tap Add.

They arrive as a new Collection and their sounds go in your Library. Nothing you already have changes. If you already have a Collection with that name, the new one gets a number, like “Party 2”. Cancel adds nothing.

What a pack carries

Each Reaction’s sound, name, colour, emoji, volume, Play range and Show name, and its GIF with its framing, key frame and timing. A Collection pack also carries the Collection’s name.

Sharing sends a copy. Editing a Reaction later doesn’t change what you already sent. The file goes only where you send it: HumFut has no server and uploads nothing.

Checked before it’s added

HumFut checks every pack on your iPhone before adding anything, including the same sound check as any sound you add. If a pack can’t be used, you see Pack not added with the reason, and nothing is added.

Your friend needs HumFut 1.5 or later to open a pack. An older version says “Update the app to open this pack.”

Back up your Library.

Save everything you’ve made to your own iCloud, and bring it back on this iPhone or a new one. It’s all in Settings → Backup & Restore.

Back up

Now, or every day

  1. Open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. Tap Back Up Now.
  3. When it’s done, you see Last backup: with the date and size.

Automatic backup backs up once a day, when you leave HumFut after changing your Library. It turns on with your first backup, and you can switch it off.

Each backup only sends what changed, and each iPhone keeps its own backup.

Restore

Bring it back

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account and open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. Tap Restore and choose a backup.
  3. Tick what you want: each Collection, Starter Pads (your edits), Sounds not on any Pad and Settings.
  4. Tap Restore Selected.

Restoring adds to your Library. Nothing on this iPhone is removed or replaced. Collections you already have say On this iPhone and start unticked.

Manage

Manage Backups

See each iPhone’s backup with its size. Yours says This iPhone.

To delete one, swipe left and tap Delete Backup. HumFut shows about how much iCloud space that frees. Anything already restored on an iPhone stays there.

No iCloud? Use a backup file

Export Backup File saves everything as one .humfutbackup file. Save it to Files, AirDrop it or send it from the share sheet.

On the other iPhone, open Settings → Backup & Restore, tap Import Backup File and choose it. You pick what to restore, as with an iCloud backup.

Anyone with the file can open it in HumFut, so keep it somewhere you trust.

What’s in a backup

  • Your Collections and Pads in order, with their names, colours, emoji, volumes and trims.
  • Every sound and recording, even ones on no Pad, and your Tracks.
  • GIFs with their framing, key frame and timing, and your edits to starter Pads.
  • Settings such as the opening tab, the Speak voice and the They hear level. They only replace this iPhone’s settings if you tick Settings.

Not included: Speak phrases, and your purchase. To get the unlock back, use Settings → Unlock HumFut → Restore Purchases. A restored starter Pad keeps this iPhone’s colour.

Backups go to your own iCloud and never to us. Privacy Policy: Backups

What HumFut is telling you.

The status at the top of HumFut always says whether the other person can hear you. These are its own words.

Checking…Confirming the call before anything can be turned on.

HumFut is checking the call. It takes a moment.

ReadySupported Call detected.

A call HumFut can use is connected. Tap Turn On.

On.Ready — tap a Reaction to play it on the call

Whatever you play now goes into the call, mixed with your voice. Stop silences everything.

On.Laugh is playing into the call.

The other person is hearing that sound right now.

Not readyCall Sounds aren’t available right now. This needs a Supported Call — and won’t work while you’re screen sharing.

There’s no call HumFut can use, or you’re sharing your screen. If you turned it on early, it starts when the call does.

Not readyHumFut doesn’t have your permission for Call audio.

Tap Open Settings and allow it.

Which calls work?

Calls your iPhone lets apps add audio to: Phone, FaceTime and calling apps like WhatsApp and Zoom. iOS decides which calls qualify. When it can, HumFut says Supported Call detected.

Good to know

  • If your mic is muted in the call app, Call Sounds are muted too, so the other person hears nothing from HumFut.
  • Sharing your screen pauses Call Sounds.
  • On a Bluetooth headset, sound goes out in mono at lower quality. Everything still plays.
  • The person on your call isn’t told: there’s no tone or announcement on their end. Keep it to sounds that are obviously not real.

Music and levels

Files longer than 30 seconds become Tracks. Play one from Library’s now-playing strip, with Play, Pause, Stop, Loop, and Mix for its level against your Reactions. It’s heard on the call when Call Sounds are on.

In the Levels sheet, They hear sets how loud everything is for the other person, and moving it changes what you hear too. What only you hear is your iPhone’s side volume buttons; HumFut never changes them.

Apple Watch

The HumFut Watch app needs watchOS 26 and your iPhone within reach. Sound always plays from your iPhone.

  • One page per Collection: tap a Pad to play or stop it.
  • Stop silences everything.
  • Turn Call Sounds on or off.
  • On the Levels page, turn the Digital Crown to set They hear, and play or pause your Track.

If your iPhone is out of reach, the Watch says “Can’t reach your iPhone. Nothing was sent.”

3 free calls, then a one-time unlock.

A call counts once sound actually goes into it. Turning on before a call starts, or off and on again in the same call, doesn’t use another. Settings → Unlock HumFut shows how many you’ve used.

After 3 calls, Turn On opens Unlock unlimited calls. It’s a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and it lets callers hear everything from HumFut on every call: Reactions, your sounds, music and typed Speak phrases. Trying Pads on your own speaker and editing your Library stay free.

The same unlock lets you put your own GIFs behind your Pads. Choosing a GIF before you’ve unlocked opens Unlock GIF backgrounds instead.

Restore a purchase: Settings → Unlock HumFut → Restore Purchases.

Fix a problem.

The other person can’t hear my sounds.

Check that HumFut’s status says On. and the call is connected. Then check that your mic isn’t muted in the call app (that mutes Call Sounds too) and that you aren’t sharing your screen.

If it says “Your last call ended. Turn Call Sounds on again for this call.”, tap Turn On: Call Sounds turn off at the end of every call.

I can’t hear what I’m sending.

Your media volume may be down. HumFut says “Media volume is down — you can’t hear what you’re sending. Use the volume buttons on the side.”

HumFut doesn’t show up when I tap Share.

HumFut appears when what you share is an audio file or a HumFut pack (.humfut). On a soundboard site, open the sound’s own page and use its Share button. If HumFut says “Open HumFut once to finish setting up, then share this again.”, do just that.

I shared a sound but it isn’t in my Library.

Shared sounds finish adding when you next open HumFut. Open the app and look in Library for the sound marked New.

My capture didn’t keep anything.

If HumFut says “Nothing was captured”, start again and play the sound while the red indicator is showing. Capture only keeps audio from other apps, and it stops by itself at 60 seconds.

Capture says I’m on a call, but I’m not.

Capture can’t start during a live or ringing call, on purpose: HumFut never keeps sound from a call. When the call ends, HumFut checks again, and it also checks when you come back to HumFut and when you open Capture.

If it still says you’re on a call, make sure the call has ended in every calling app, then close Capture and open it again. Before HumFut 1.6, Capture could stay blocked after a call until HumFut was closed, so update from the App Store if you haven’t.

Why won’t HumFut add a sound?

HumFut checks every sound on your iPhone and won’t add one that sounds like an emergency siren, an automated phone menu or dial tones, or a phone ringtone, because playing it into a call could be taken as real. Tap What’s this? in HumFut to read more, or choose another file.

“HumFut can’t play this file.” or “This file is damaged and won’t play.” means the file itself won’t open. Try a different audio file.

“HumFut couldn’t set up its audio.”

Turn Call Sounds off and on again. If it happens again, another audio app may be holding the audio: close it, or restart HumFut.

My GIF Pad isn’t moving.

A GIF Pad plays once in slow motion as it comes into view, then rests on its key frame until you tap it. It plays again after it leaves the screen and comes back.

GIF Pads show their still when Reduce Motion is on, in Low Power Mode, or when Auto-Play Animated Images is off in Settings → Accessibility → Motion. Under Reduce Motion a tap plays the sound without moving the GIF; in Low Power Mode a tap plays the GIF once at normal speed.

On a Board with lots of GIF Pads, up to six play their slow-motion pass at once, or four when your iPhone is warm. The others wait their turn and play as each one finishes. When your iPhone is warm, GIFs move a little less smoothly to keep it cool. When it’s running hot, only the 4 Pads nearest the middle of the screen move. Taps still play.

Why won’t my GIF add?

HumFut tells you why, and your Pad stays as it was:

  • “That GIF is over 8 MB. Choose a smaller one.”
  • “That isn’t a GIF.” The file or copied item is another kind of picture or a video. Choose or copy a GIF itself.
  • “That’s a web page, not a GIF — copy the GIF itself.” The page has no GIF HumFut can use. Open the GIF and copy it, or copy its own link.
  • “Only secure (https) links work.” or “Couldn’t reach that link. Check it and try again.” Check the link, and that you’re online.
A pack I was sent won’t open.

HumFut shows Pack not added with the reason, and adds nothing. For example:

  • “Update the app to open this pack.” Update HumFut from the App Store, then tap the file again.
  • “This file is damaged or isn’t a HumFut pack.” Ask for it to be sent again.
  • “This pack is too big.”, “This pack has more than 12 Reactions.” or “A sound in this pack is longer than 60 seconds.” Ask for a smaller Collection.
  • “HumFut won’t add this pack. A sound in it sounds like…” A sound failed the same check as any sound you add.

If another sheet is open when a pack arrives, Add Reactions waits and opens when you close it.

How do I delete a Collection?

In Settings → Manage Collections, swipe left on the Collection and tap Delete, or tap Edit. The starter Collection has its own Delete Collection button.

If it has Reactions, HumFut asks what to do with them: Delete Reactions and their sounds & GIFs, Delete Reactions, keep sounds in Library, or Cancel. A sound or GIF that another Reaction still uses is never removed. This can’t be undone, and deleted starter Reactions don’t come back.

How do I delete a sound or a Pad?

To delete a sound, swipe left on it in Library and tap Delete. If it’s on a Pad, that Pad goes too, with its GIF. To take a sound off Reactions but keep it, hold its Pad and tap Remove, the trash can at the top. To keep the Pad but drop its GIF, tap Remove GIF. Settings → Storage shows the space your sounds use and can remove the starter sounds.

Back Up Now, Restore or Manage Backups is greyed out.

They need iCloud Drive. HumFut says what’s missing:

  • “Sign in to iCloud to back up”: open the Settings app, tap Sign in at the top, then turn on iCloud Drive.
  • “Turn on iCloud Drive for HumFut to back up”: open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then iCloud Drive, and switch on HumFut.

Export Backup File and Import Backup File work without iCloud.

It says “Still uploading to iCloud”.

The backup is saved on your iPhone, and iCloud is still sending it. It isn’t backed up until that finishes. iCloud uploads in its own time, so stay connected to the internet and check again later; the line changes to Last backup: once it’s there.

If it says your iCloud storage is full, free up iCloud storage, or delete an old backup in Manage Backups. You can also use Export Backup File.

My other iPhone’s backup isn’t listed.

Check that both iPhones use the same Apple Account, and that the other iPhone’s backup has finished uploading: on that iPhone, Backup & Restore should show Last backup:, not “Still uploading”.

A backup from another iPhone can take a few minutes to appear. Tap Look Again in a moment.

Something didn’t come back after a restore.

A restore adds only what you tick. Collections already on the iPhone start unticked; tick one anyway and you get a second copy with a number, like “Party 2”. Speak phrases and your purchase aren’t in backups: use Restore Purchases in Settings → Unlock HumFut.

HumFut tells you if anything was missing from the backup, for example “2 Pads were restored without their GIF because it was missing from the backup.” or “1 Pad wasn’t restored because its sound was missing from the backup”. If a sound couldn’t be read when you backed up, HumFut said so then, and it isn’t in that backup.

“This backup is from a newer version of HumFut” means you need to update the app from the App Store first.

What’s the Speak tab?

Type what you want to say and tap Speak: HumFut speaks it into the call on your iPhone. It needs Call Sounds on. Your pinned phrases and recent ones are kept for next time.

Can I try new features before they’re released?

Yes. Upcoming versions go to beta testers on TestFlight first. Join the beta with your Apple Account email and we’ll send you an invite.

Does HumFut record my calls?

No. HumFut never uses your microphone, never records a call and never listens to one. Capture is separate: it only keeps audio from another app that you start and stop yourself. Read the Privacy Policy
